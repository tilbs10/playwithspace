## The Setup

Only four girls at training. After the warm-up, every drill came back to one idea: protecting the ball.

## What We Worked On

**The pivot, and ear to back pocket.** When the pressure arrives, pivot, and move the ball from your ear to your back pocket — away from the defender, where she can't get a hand on it. It's the simplest way to keep the ball when someone's in your space, and it's the starting point for everything we want to do with it.

**Three ways in.** We built it up in steps:

- **Off the dribble** — stop, pivot, protect.
- **Off the pass** — catch, pivot, protect.
- **With a jump stop and a finish at the hoop** — stop under control, pivot, and finish.

Same skill each time, arriving from a different place. The game won't hand them the pivot in one neat situation, so we practised it from the three they'll actually meet.

**The rebound and the outlet box.** We only had a brief chance at the end, but we worked on securing the rebound and introduced the outlet box: a known spot to send the first pass, so the ball gets out of the crowd under the basket and the break can start.

## What It Teaches

**Protecting the ball buys time, and time buys space.** In [Game 2](/blog/game-2-hands-up-eyes-open) the spacing got better because our stronger players could handle the pressure, and that bought everyone else time to get to their spots. The pivot is how every player learns to do that, not just the strongest. A girl who can keep the ball under pressure doesn't have to panic-pass or dribble into trouble; she can look up and find the room.

**A pivot is a decision, not just footwork.** Ear to back pocket protects the ball, but it also turns you to face the floor. Off the dribble, off the pass or at the hoop, the pivot is the moment you get to see your options.

**The outlet box is the first piece of transition.** A secured rebound and a pass to a spot everyone knows: that's how a defensive stop becomes a [fast break](/blog/passing-ahead-in-transition). We've only introduced it; it'll take reminders before it's a habit.

## Next Up

The game. I'll be reminding them about the outlet box throughout — every defensive rebound is a chance to find it.
