## The Setup

The day after [Game 4](/blog/game-4-denied). The game left me with a short list: the ball kept stopping, decisions were slow, and a lot of easy shots didn't go in.

So the session had three jobs: fix the easy misses, get the girls comfortable making quick decisions, and teach them to pass on the move so the ball keeps going.

## What We Worked On

**A baseline shooting drill.** We started here because of the easy misses in the game. Close shots that should have gone in, and didn't. Before anything about spacing or movement matters, a team has to finish the chances it creates.

**The three-girl weave.** Three players moving down the floor, passing and cutting behind the pass. It's the simplest way to practise passing on the move: the ball never stops, and nobody gets to stand and wait for it. It also keeps three lanes filled, so nobody bunches.

**A UCLA-style layup drill.** The main event. It's a constant-movement drill, and it rolls several skills into one flow: layups, the outlet box, and passing down the floor. Players don't get to stop and think. They receive, move, pass and finish, over and over, which is as close to quick decisions under tempo as a drill gets.

**A ten-minute scrimmage against the B team.** To finish, we played. Ten minutes, against a team that wasn't ours, so the girls could try it against someone who wasn't a teammate.

## What It Teaches

**Momentum is a skill.** In Game 4 the ball arrived to someone standing still, and everything stopped. The weave and the UCLA drill are the opposite: movement doesn't pause between passes. Practising that rhythm is how it starts to feel normal.

**Pieces connect.** The outlet box from [Session 2](/blog/session-2-protect-the-ball) is in the layup drill now, as one link in a chain, rather than something to remember on its own. That's what the [transition](/blog/passing-ahead-in-transition) game needs: a rebound, an outlet, a pass down the floor and a finish.

**Finishing comes first.** It would have been easy to skip the shooting and jump straight to the clever stuff. But a good possession that ends in an easy miss is just a wasted possession.

## Next Up

Game 5. I'll be watching whether the quick decisions and the moving passes carry over, and whether the finishing improves.
