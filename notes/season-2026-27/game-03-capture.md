# Game 3 — Capture

- Date: 2026-10-25
- Result: W 28-12
- Players: 2 fill-ins covering for 2 regulars who had other obligations
- Season: 2026-27

## Raw notes (from the coach)

We had 2 new players help by filling in for 2 girls that had other
obligations, this meant a lot that we had done at the previous training had
to be postponed and we focus on the simple things of getting back on
defense, keeping our hands straight up and forcing the other team into bad
decisions by continous pressure. The girls are starting to comprehend the
reason for getting back on defense, they still will come out to meet the
ball once the other team gets over halfway, this is leaving space behind for
the opposition to attack, which is usually off the dribble, there are some
of the girls who our realising the key areas and are beating the opposition
to that spot, but until they can control the attackers dribble it is
exposing too much space behind them, the target is to play good hands up
defense and teach the girls off the ball to read the next pass, this
happened in the game in the 2nd half by one player picking up 2 steals off
the exact play. This type of instinct and awareness will be the foundation
of 3/4 court and 1/2 court presses, which should lead to some fast break
opportunities
