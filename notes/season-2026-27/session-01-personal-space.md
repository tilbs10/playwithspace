# Session 1 — Finding Your Own Space With the Ball

**Theme:** Personal space — how to find room when you have the ball and everyone feels close
**Players:** 4 (U12 girls, Year 4s — 2 with basketball experience, 2 new from netball)
**Duration:** 60 mins
**Equipment:** Half court, 4 balls, cones (6–8), a few spots/markers
**Why this session:** Game 1 (L 6-38) showed a group that felt crowded all game —
they walked toward the ball and the defence and stood where the defenders
were. Before spacing *as a team* comes spacing *as a person*: can you find
room around yourself?

---

## The Question to Hold All Session

*"Where is the room, and how do you get to it?"*

Keep it to that one idea. No team spacing, no plays, no defence concepts
today — those come later.

---

## Warm-up — 8 mins

**Free dribble in the half court, everyone with a ball**
- Walk, then jog. Eyes up — "find a patch of floor nobody is using"
- Every 30 seconds, call **"Freeze."** Players stop, ball held, and look around:
  *"Who has the most room? Who's got a neighbour close by?"*
- Shrink the area with cones every round (full half court → key plus a bit → a
  small box). Crowding appears by itself — that's the point
- Coaching cue: *"Find your bubble."* A bubble is the room around you where
  nobody else is
- Short breath check at the end of the warm-up: one slow breath, shoulders
  down. Not a speech — just practise noticing

---

## Block 1 — 12 mins
*Instruction (2 mins) + Game (10 mins)*

**Concept:** Moving toward room, not toward people

**Instruction (2 mins):** Hold a ball and walk toward a teammate. Ask:
*"What's it feel like when someone's right on top of you? What could you do?"*
Let them answer — step away, turn, move the other way. Name it afterwards:
**go where the room is.**

**Game: Bubble tag-free dribble**
- Everyone dribbling in the small box. No tagging, no defender — just don't
  get closer than an arm's length to anyone
- If someone is in your bubble, the dribbler *changes direction* toward open floor
- Round 2: add a cone "island" in the middle that nobody can enter
- Round 3: take one ball away so one player dribbles while three move around
  her — the dribbler finds gaps, the other three drift and don't crowd
- Coaching cue: *"Head up. Where's the gap? Go."*
- Ask once, mid-drill: *"What did you see just before you changed direction?"*

---

## Block 2 — 12 mins
*Instruction (2 mins) + Drill (10 mins)*

**Concept:** Protecting space with the ball — and getting comfortable with contact

Game 1's physicality was new to them, so build contact up in small steps.
Never rush this block.

**Drill: Escape the circle (1 on 1, passive then light pressure)**
- Ball-handler in a cone circle, one defender outside
- **Round 1 — shadow only:** defender walks alongside, hands behind her back.
  Ball-handler practises pivoting and shielding the ball with her body,
  turning away from the defender
- **Round 2 — mirror:** defender mirrors but may not touch the ball.
  Ball-handler finds the gap and leaves the circle with a dribble
- **Round 3 — light pressure** (only if the group is ready): defender may
  reach, no grabbing. Ball-handler's only job is to keep her body between the
  ball and the defender
- Coaching cue: *"Turn your back to the pressure. The ball is on the far hip."*
- Check in on nerves: *"How did your body feel when she got close?"* Normalise it

---

## Block 3 — 10 mins
*Instruction (2 mins) + Drill (8 mins)*

**Concept:** When there's no room, pass out of it — *(this is the bridge to next
session; keep it light)*

Game 1: dribbling dominated, and passing is where the big gain is. Don't teach
passing technique yet — just plant the idea that a pass is another way to
escape a crowd.

**Drill: Pairs "escape pass"**
- Two players, one defender passively shadowing the ball-handler
- Ball-handler is "stuck" — can't find room with the dribble. She looks for
  her partner and passes
- Receiver *finds her own bubble* before the pass arrives (links to Block 1)
- If a pass is wasted, ask: *"Was she in a bubble when you passed?"*
- Optional: the two with netball backgrounds can demonstrate catching and
  passing without dribbling — they already know that game

---

## Live Game — 10 mins

**2v2 half court, rotate players so everyone plays with and against everyone**
- One coaching rule only: **before you dribble, take one look** — find your
  bubble, then go
- Let them play. Minimal interruption — stop play only for a "freeze" moment
  if two players are bunched up
- Scoring: normal. No special points today — keep the focus on the look and the room

**End question (sit down, water, one at a time):**
*"When did you find room today without being told to?"*

---

## Coach's Notes

- Who finds the open floor naturally, and who gets pulled toward the ball?
- Who looks like she's about to panic when someone gets close? (Note — don't
  fix it, note it.)
- Did the "freeze" moments change how anyone moved?
- Did the two netball players pass more readily than they dribbled?
- One thing to carry into session 2 — likely: *"Where do you stand when you
  don't have the ball?"*
