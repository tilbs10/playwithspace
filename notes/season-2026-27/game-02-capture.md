# Game 2 — Capture

- Date: 2026-10-18
- Result: W 50-6
- Players: 6 (strongest player rejoined)
- Season: 2026-27

## Raw notes (from the coach)

Second game: 6 players, strongest player rejoined, solid victory 50-6. Other
team very weak, only one player with any skills. Focus became defensive
transition and good hands-up defence — not reaching in and causing a foul,
looking to make the read on the next pass and look for the interception.
During the 4th quarter we finally got to this and got fast break points
from it. This skill will develop into the foundation of our half-court
press. Improvement in court spacing because our stronger players were able
to handle and mitigate the pressure, which bought time to get to spots.
