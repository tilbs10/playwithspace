- Date: 2026-11-19
- Result: W 12-10
- Opponent: the top team — the team we lost to in game 1 (L 6-38) and game 4 (L 17-25)
- Season: 2026-27

## Raw notes (from the coach)

Last game was on the 19/11 and we won, 12-10 against the top team, which we
have lost our only games to. It was a tough game, it was poorly officiated,
which put a lot of pressure on the girls. The opposition was used to winning
quite easily and due to the close score really stepped up how aggressively
they were playing. We were able to manage, we only used the screen away which
we worked on at training once but it was effective. Our best player was under
the most attention the whole game and highlighted the importance of the other
girls getting to the right spots on the court. The moment that stood out was
the 2nd qtr when we got down and after a time out responded with better
spacing and were able to come back and take the half time lead. Things to
work on: defensive shape, understanding where the parts of the court are we
want to prevent driving lanes; I will also put in a press break that we can
practice before playing this team again. There are only 4 teams so we will
have a few games to work on getting the full court spacing and timing right,
also the types of passes required to get the ball down the court.
