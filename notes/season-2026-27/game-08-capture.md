## Raw notes (from the coach)

Last game we won 35 to 30, it was a good game with one of the opposing
players being far taller. It put pressure on our shot selection: where
normally we would have a shot or layup, that was often taken away.
Throughout the game naturally the drive, draw and dish it off became our
best option. The defensive rebounds were also important; limiting their 2nd
chance opportunities became critical, and our focus on protecting the ball
once it is secured: keeping the ball high or at their chin and using their
pivot to find an outlet pass. All the girls contributed in a strong game and
are consistently showing improvement through the course of the game.
