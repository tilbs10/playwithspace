# Session 5 — Breaking the Press

**Theme:** Get the ball in, get it to the middle, and get it down the floor
against full-court pressure — with passes, not dribbles
**Date:** TBC (before the next game against the top team)
**Players:** Squad — built for 5; notes below for 4 or 6
**Duration:** 60 mins
**Equipment:** Full court, a ball per pair, cones (8–10) to mark the press-break
spots and the trap zones, bibs for the defence
**Why this session:** Game 7 (W 12-10) against the top team: an aggressive,
in-your-face side that lifted its pressure as soon as the score was close, with
our best player denied all game. They'll press us harder next time. A press
only works if we panic — dribble into a corner, throw a rushed pass, or all
come to the ball. This session gives every player a spot, a job and a pass.

---

## The Question to Hold All Session

*"Where is the middle?"*

A press wants the ball on the sideline and in the corners, where the lines
do the defending. The middle of the floor is where the press is weakest,
because a pass from there can go either way. Every rule today comes back to
getting the ball there, or past it.

---

## The Press Break — Shape and Rules

Five spots. Walk it before you run it.

```
            (their basket — where we're going)
   ------------------------------------------------
   |                    4                         |   4  LONG: deep, near halfway
   |                                              |      on the far side. Stretches
   |----------------------------------------------|      the press; the pass over
   |                    3                         |      the top if it's there.
   |                                              |   3  MIDDLE: centre circle.
   |          1                    2              |      Flashes to the ball once
   |                                              |      it's in.
   |                                              |   1, 2  CUTTERS: start at the
   |                                              |      elbows, cut hard to the
   ------------------5 (inbounds)------------------      sideline to get open.
            (our basket — we inbound here)            5  INBOUNDER: once the ball
                                                          is in, steps in behind it
                                                          as the safety (trail).
```

**The four rules:**

1. **Get it in.** Cutters 1 and 2 cut to opposite sides — never to the same
   spot. V-cut: go away first, then come back to the ball hard, hands up.
2. **Get to the middle.** As soon as the ball is in, 3 flashes to the ball side
   of the middle and calls for it. The inbounder steps in as the safety behind
   the ball, so there's always a pass back.
3. **Pass ahead, don't dribble into it.** Two quick passes beat a press.
   One dribble towards the sideline is how you get trapped.
4. **If you're trapped: pivot, protect, find the middle or the safety.**
   Ear to back pocket (Session 2). Don't pick up the dribble near a corner.
   Don't throw it in a hurry — the trap is two defenders, so somebody is open.

Once the ball crosses halfway, we're into the half court and back to our
spots. A press break is only the first ten seconds.

---

## Warm-up — 8 mins

**The weekly drills: three-girl weave, full court**
- Two trips each, then add the rule: no dribbling at all
- Coaching cue: *"Pass ahead of her, so she runs onto it."*
- This is the press break in disguise — the ball moves faster than a defender
  can run

---

## Block 1 — 10 mins
*Instruction (2 mins) + Drill (8 mins)*

**Concept:** The right pass for the pressure

Three passes, one job each. Show each once, then go.
- **Bounce pass:** past a defender's hands when her hands are up — aim
  two-thirds of the way to your teammate
- **Overhead pass:** over the top of a trap, or long to number 4
- **Chest pass:** on the move, to a teammate who's open with nobody between

**Drill: Pass past the hand (3 players: passer, defender, receiver)**
- Defender stands between, hands up (our own hands-up defence from Game 2)
- Passer pivots to find a window and chooses the pass: bounce if the hands are
  high, overhead if they're low
- Receiver steps to the ball and calls for it
- Rotate every 5 passes
- Coaching cue: *"Where are her hands? Pass where they aren't."*

---

## Block 2 — 10 mins
*Instruction (2 mins) + Drill (8 mins)*

**Concept:** Get it in — inbounding against pressure

**Drill: 2v2 plus the inbounder**
- Inbounder on the baseline, two cutters, two defenders face-guarding them
- Coach counts five seconds out loud — that's the rule they'll face in a game
- Cutters V-cut to opposite sides; the inbounder passes to the one who's open
  and steps in as the safety
- Round 2: add the flash — a third player starts at halfway and comes to the
  middle once the ball is in
- Coaching cue (inbounder): *"Look first. You have five seconds — use three."*
- Coaching cue (cutters): *"Go away, then come back. Same side as her is a turnover."*

---

## Block 3 — 17 mins
*Walk (5 mins) + Run (12 mins)*

**Concept:** The whole press break

**Walk it, 5v0 (5 mins)**
- Set the five spots with cones. Walk the ball in, to the middle, ahead, over
  halfway
- Do it from both sides: inbound to 1's side, then to 2's side
- Stop on each pass and ask: *"Where's the middle now?"*

**Run it against pressure (12 mins)**
- 5v3 first: three defenders press — two on the cutters, one roaming to trap.
  The offence always has two free players; the job is to find them
- Then 5v4, then 5v5 if the group is ready
- Mark the two trap zones with cones (the sideline corners each side of
  halfway). Rule: *a pass out of a trap zone is worth a point to the offence;
  a dribble into one is a point to the defence*
- Coaching cue: *"Two passes and we're through."*

**If you have 4:** drop number 4 (long) — 3 cutters/middle plus the inbounder
**If you have 6:** the extra player joins the defence, so the break is always
against real pressure

---

## Live Game — 15 mins

**Full court, defence presses after every made basket**
- 5v5 if numbers allow, or play the B team if they're there
- Every inbound starts from the press-break shape
- One rule only: **no dribbling in the back court until the ball has been
  passed once**
- Let them play. Stop only to ask *"where was the middle?"* after a turnover

**End question (sit down, water, one at a time):**
*"When the pressure came, where did you find space?"*

---

## Coach's Notes

- Who cut to the same spot as a teammate? (The most common mistake — fix the
  cut, not the pass)
- Did the inbounder look, or throw the first pass available?
- Who panicked in a trap, and who pivoted out of it?
- Did 3 flash to the middle, or wait to be found?
- Which pass did each player choose — and was it the right one for the
  defender's hands?
- One thing to carry into the next session — likely: *defensive shape*,
  protecting the driving lanes (the other focus from Game 7)
