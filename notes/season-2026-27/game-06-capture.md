# Game 6 — Capture

- Date: 2026-11-12
- Result: W 42-14 (corrected by the coach; first noted as 53-6)
- Season: 2026-27

## Raw notes (from the coach)

We won our last game 53 to 6. It was a good game. We started off as an
aggressive team, prompted pre-game by the way they were going to be, right
in the girls' faces. The use of the pivot, where they place the ball and
place their body as the construction.

The first quarter was pretty even. We couldn't really get going. Just the
initial pressure was quite high. Once the girls started to become more
comfortable, we got cleaner rebounds, started to create a few fast break
opportunities, and they started to relax in the game. They played a lot
better.

Still the catch from the rebound was the main concern: bringing the ball
down after they rebounded it. That's what we'll focus on next training:
taking the rebound, pivoting, scanning the floor, and looking for a good
outlet pass. A lot of our outlet passes were rushed and I want to, in
preparation for the next game, continue our work on the screen away.

## Corrections and additions (from the coach)

Score was 42-14, not 53-6. We were preparing for their aggression: quite an
in-your-face defensive team. The change probably came in the second
quarter: we kept our shape better on defence and adjusted to the initial
defensive pressure.
